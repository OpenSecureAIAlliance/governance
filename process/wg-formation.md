# Open Secure AI Alliance — Working Group Formation and Governance

---

## 1. Purpose

The Open Secure AI Alliance (OSAIA) establishes working groups to advance specific areas of its mission through collaboration among Alliance members. OSAIA may maintain multiple working groups, each with a defined mission, scope, charter, and expected outcomes.

---

## 2. Proposing a New Working Group

Any OSAIA member may propose a new working group for consideration by the OSAIA Steering Committee.

The proposal must include:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The proposed working group's name |
| **Mission** | The purpose of the group and how it advances OSAIA's mission |
| **Focus and scope** | The topics the group will address, including any boundaries or overlap with existing working groups |
| **Draft charter** | The group's objectives, expected deliverables, and governance framework |
| **Proposed interim leadership** | Suggested candidates for Interim Chair and Interim Co-chair (from different organizations), including their Alliance affiliations |
| **Initial participation** | Known interested members and a plan to invite participation across the Alliance |
| **Initial operating plan** | Proposed activities, meeting cadence, and communication channels |
| **Expected duration** | Length of time to achieve working group objectives (default: 1 year, renewable) |

Use the template at [`templates/WG_NAME_proposal.md`](./templates/WG_NAME_proposal.md). Submit the completed proposal by opening a pull request that adds it to [`wg-proposals/`](./wg-proposals/).

---

## 3. Steering Committee Review and Approval

The Steering Committee or delegated representatives will review each proposal for alignment with OSAIA's mission, clarity of scope, potential value, and coordination with existing working groups.

The Steering Committee may request revisions before voting. Creation of a working group requires approval through a Steering Committee vote conducted under its established voting rules.

Upon approval, the Steering Committee will approve the initial charter and select an Interim Chair and Interim Co-chair. The Steering Committee will give good-faith consideration to the candidates proposed in the submission.

Working groups will create and share reports, at least once annually, on their activities, accomplishments, and key focus areas for Steering Committee review. The Steering Committee may vote to extend or terminate an established working group at any time.

---

## 4. Membership and Participation

Any OSAIA member may join any OSAIA working group. Participation is not restricted to founding members or to members affiliated with the Chair or Co-chair.

For purposes of this policy, a working group member is an OSAIA member organization represented by a designated individual listed on the working group's membership roster. Each working group member has one vote.

---

## 5. Interim Leadership

The Steering Committee will appoint the Interim Chair and Interim Co-chair (from different organizations) for an initial period of six weeks, beginning on the working group's establishment date.

During this period, interim leadership will:

- Invite participation from across the Alliance
- Establish the membership roster, meeting schedule, and communication channels
- Convene initial meetings and develop a work plan
- Organize nominations and the first leadership election

---

## 6. Leadership Elections and Terms

At the end of the six-week interim period, working group members will select a Chair and Co-chair for 1-year terms, renewable with no term limit.

Any OSAIA member is eligible to serve as Chair or Co-chair. Interim leaders may stand for election. Chair and Co-chair must be from different organizations.

Elections are decided by plurality vote: the candidate with the highest vote count becomes Chair and the candidate with the second-highest vote count becomes Co-chair. Ties are first resolved among the tied candidates themselves; if no resolution is reached, the matter is elevated to the Steering Committee.

The membership roster and election schedule must be communicated before voting begins. Members must have a reasonable opportunity to nominate candidates and cast their votes.

If either position remains unfilled when the interim period ends, the matter will be referred to the Steering Committee to arrange temporary leadership and a new election.

If either leadership position becomes vacant during a term, the Steering Committee may appoint an interim replacement pending an election conducted under this section.

---

## 7. Charter Changes

A working group's charter defines its approved mission, scope, objectives, and governance framework.

Any change to a working group's charter may be proposed as a pull request to the public charter document or as a change request to the Steering Committee. A Steering Committee representative may accept the change directly, or escalate to a full Steering Committee vote in the case of substantial proposed changes.

---

## 8. Programs and Operations

Each working group is responsible for creating, managing, and modifying its programs, activities, and operating plans within its approved charter.

The Chair and Co-chair may periodically propose changes for consideration by the working group. They must provide the full working group with notice of proposed changes, including a description and a reasonable deadline for members to raise concerns.

- **No concerns raised** — the Chair and Co-chair may implement the changes.
- **Concern raised** — the Chair and Co-chair will facilitate discussion to resolve it.

This operational process does not authorize charter changes. Any proposal that would amend the charter must follow the process in Section 7.

---

## 9. Leadership Responsibilities and Accountability

The Chair and Co-chair will coordinate the working group's activities, facilitate meetings, maintain agendas and records, track deliverables, and report progress to the Steering Committee.

Each working group will maintain its approved charter, leadership terms, meeting records, work plan, and operational collateral in the working group's GitHub repository.

The Chair and Co-chair will lead the creation and sharing of working group reports, at least once annually, to the Steering Committee.
